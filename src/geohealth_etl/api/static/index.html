Themes included in the burden score

How is the burden score calculated?
  1. Rank each factor (0–100). Every tract is ranked against the other Middlesex tracts on each environmental factor. The rank is flipped where needed so that 100 always means the worst condition: most traffic and pollution, but least tree canopy, open space or transit. A percentile of 80 means worse than about 80% of tracts.
  2. Average within each theme. A theme score is the plain average of its factors' percentiles, e.g. Green space = (tree canopy + open space + distance to open space) ÷ 3.
  3. Average the themes, then rank. The burden score is the average of the ticked theme scores, so each theme counts equally however many factors it has. The map colours tracts by the rank of that average: the darkest 20% are the most burdened fifth of the county.

Who lives in a tract (race, immigration, income) and the modelled mental-health estimates are not part of the score; they're compared with it in the box below. Percentiles are relative to this county only, not to any health standard.
